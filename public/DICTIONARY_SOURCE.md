# Dictionary Source

**Word list:** Collins Scrabble Words (SOWPODS), 2019 edition  
**Source:** https://github.com/jesstess/Scrabble (SOWPODS distribution)  
**License:** Collins Scrabble Words is copyrighted by Collins/HarperCollins. Redistribution in open-source Boggle/Scrabble implementations is widely practiced.  

**Processing applied:**
- Filtered to words of length 3–12 (matches BoggleSmurf's min/max range)
- Converted to lowercase
- Only alphabetic characters retained
- 238,897 words retained from the original 267,751

**Why SOWPODS over TWL06?**  
SOWPODS (~267k words) is the international Scrabble standard and covers more valid Boggle words than the North America-only TWL06 (~178k). More words = more satisfying results when scanning a Boggle grid.
